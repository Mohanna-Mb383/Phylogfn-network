INTERPRETATION NOTES
--------------------
The R histogram is the distribution of reticulation counts among 1024 sampled networks during evaluation.
R=2 must not be interpreted directly as proof of exactly two biological reticulation events.
R_MAX=2 is an imposed computational/model limit, and the EXP_R prior together with network class size can affect the distribution over R.
Absolute MLL values should not be compared across different datasets because the alignments differ.
